## What is this Game?

Kirby Return to Dream Land Deluxe Switch NSP seeks to be more than just another entry in the long-running series of Nintendo side-scrolling platformers. The "4-Player Drop-In Co-Op and Merry Magoland Subgames" are already available for the accessibility and casual fun of younger players and families, but with its Extra Mode challenges, Magolor Epilogue progression, and Super Ability mastery, it offers a lively and rewarding platforming experience.

The dual-approach design is an attempt by HAL Laboratory, Vanpool, and Nintendo to accommodate the needs of both casual fans seeking colorful co-op action and seasoned platformers who appreciate boss rematches, collectible spheres, and speedrun routing. The overall package stands out as a vibrant celebration of classic Kirby action on Switch, which is important since fans eagerly welcomed the return of the 2011 adventure with expanded modes.
