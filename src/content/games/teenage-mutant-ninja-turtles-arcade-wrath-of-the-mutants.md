## What is this Game?

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Arcade Wrath of the Mutants Switch NSP seeks to be more than just another entry in the long-running series of arcade beat-'em-up titles. The "Simple Combo Controls and Turtle Power Ultimates" are already available for the accessibility and casual fun of newer fans, but with its demanding stage bosses, high-score leaderboards, and fast-paced Foot Clan waves, it offers a deeply technical and entertaining brawler experience.

The dual-approach design is an attempt by GameMill Entertainment and Raw Thrills to accommodate the needs of both casual players seeking coin-op style party fun and hardcore beat-'em-up veterans who appreciate position spacing, pickup timing, and clear-time optimizations. The overall package delivers classic arcade action to home consoles, which is important since fans wanted easy access to this previously arcade-exclusive entry.
