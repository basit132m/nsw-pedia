## What is DataDock Host?

DataDock Host is a free files sharing platform, neither managed nor handled by us. Here the DataDock Host team shares the files that can be shared anywhere by anyone. They provide a direct file sharing link which downlaods the respective file.

## How to Download from DataDock host?

As this platform is not connected to us, we don't take any of the responsibility of them. They can use the ads later on if they want, its their choice. But according to them to run this complex system of files sharing they need a amount of money to keep the servers live.

Get straight to the point, How to download from their!

So, right now as per their guide, to download a file from their platform you need to click the download button 3 times. First click will opens up the ad, second will also opens up the ad. And on third click your file download will starts.

## Reason of Using DataDock Host:

Reason behind using this amazing platform is that its free of charge for users, no download limit, separate dashboard access on demand if you want to upload your own files instead of using their uploaded ones.

One another main reason is the fast download speed of their servers. As our team research they use cloudflare object storage servers with paid workers plan.
